## Current sidebar farm prompt (`sidebar-farm.webp`)

> Use the attached screenshot as a visual reference and edit it into a standalone asset: recreate ONLY the pastoral dairy farm scene at the bottom left of the sidebar. Show a large black-and-white cow grazing in a lush sunlit meadow in the foreground, with a small warm red/orange barn, tall silver silo, trees and distant farm landscape behind it, matching the composition, natural photorealistic style and fresh green tones of that reference. This is a vertical bottom-of-sidebar decorative illustration: scene should fill the entire width and sit at the bottom, with grass across the bottom edge and a soft fade into a genuinely transparent sky at the top. Preserve the natural farm scene, but remove every part of the dashboard, text, icons, borders, bottles, logos, buttons, and all UI. No text, no watermarks. Transparent background.
