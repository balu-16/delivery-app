# Subscriptions

A subscription stores a calendar-date anchor in Asia/Kolkata and effective-dated quantity, frequency, slot and address versions. The generation worker records one occurrence per subscription and delivery date. A single database transaction locks the customer and subscription, records the occurrence, prices through checkout, claims capacity, debits the wallet, reserves stock and confirms the order. A retry cannot create a second debit. Occurrences that lack wallet funds remain retryable until the configured grace deadline; terminal failures are recorded and repeated low balance pauses the subscription.

Both customer and admin write surfaces require an idempotency key. Their mutation and replay result commit in the same database transaction. Customer changes take the account and subscription locks before reading the database clock. A named skip or unskip after cutoff is rejected; quantity, pause, resume and cancellation report their first effective date. Subscription orders whose delivery date has reached cutoff cannot be cancelled through the customer order endpoint.

The worker claims bounded store-scoped batches with short `SKIP LOCKED` transactions and expiring leases. Business execution revalidates the lease after locking the customer and subscription; expired claims recover on a later scan. A shared worker limit bounds concurrent business transactions. The worker reconciles missed runs and records past slots as skipped. It forecasts low wallet balance, publishes durable user notifications, and reports price increases above the configured threshold. Generation evaluates store serviceability and inventory against the delivery slot. Confirmed orders retain stock, pick tasks are released at their configured lead time, and dispatch cannot run before its own lead time. A pick shortage cancels the entire subscription order through the existing compensation path.

`SUBSCRIPTION_OBLIGATIONS` lets erasure check active and paused subscriptions under the customer account lock. Address deletion invokes the independent `ADDRESS_SUBSCRIPTIONS` provider to pause affected subscriptions and publish notifications in the deletion transaction.

Focused checks: `pnpm test:unit --testPathPatterns subscriptions`; `pnpm test:int --testPathPatterns scheduled-checkout` with test PostgreSQL and Redis configured. The distributed funded load check runs with `RUN_SUBSCRIPTION_DISTRIBUTED_LOAD=1 pnpm test:int --testPathPatterns distributed-load`. The opt-in holiday and single-wallet hotspot checks use `RUN_SUBSCRIPTION_LOAD=1 pnpm test:int --testPathPatterns batch-load`.

The customer subscription list and monthly calendar, and admin plans, slots, holidays, and generation runs, return bounded `{ items, nextCursor }` pages. Cursors are bound to the user, month, or store filter where applicable.

The pinned Docker acceptance run generated 10,000 wallet-funded orders across ten stores and ten wallets in 488.238 seconds with Node 26.13.0 and PostgreSQL 16.15. It verified one dated occurrence, order, wallet debit, reservation and capacity claim per subscription after replay. The earlier pinned single-store, single-wallet hotspot run exceeded its 950-second test timeout under concurrent regression load; its failure evidence is retained separately in `artifacts/p10-p12-final-acceptance/load-contention-baseline.*`. The distributed result does not establish the hotspot performance target.
