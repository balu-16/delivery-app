# Store portal artwork

The section images in `public/images/store/` were generated with the built-in image-generation tool, then optimized as transparent WebP files. `sidebar-farm.webp` is the existing farm illustration used by the admin portal.

## Shared prompt

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: decorative hero image beside the heading of a DairyDirect store-manager web portal. Create one wide horizontal scene in a coherent premium dairy illustration style, softly photorealistic with polished realistic 3D details, natural warm daylight and a fresh green, ivory and soft gold palette. The visual language should match a sunny dairy-farm admin dashboard. Place the subject toward the right with breathing room; keep the full scene in frame with soft feathered edges. Genuine transparent background, no rectangular canvas. No interface, no logos, no text, no numbers, no watermark, no personal details.

Each image adds the following subject to the shared prompt:

| --- | --- |

| `inventory.webp` | An orderly dairy shop refrigerated shelf with fresh milk bottles, yogurt jars and tidy green stock crates, communicating stock control. |
| `returns.webp` | A neatly returned dairy parcel and glass milk bottle with a magnifying glass and small checkmark on a clipboard, clearly communicating careful product quality inspection. |
